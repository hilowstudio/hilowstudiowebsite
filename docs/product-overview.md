# LinkedIn Content Manager

## What It Does

This is a complete system for creating, scheduling, and publishing LinkedIn content that sounds like you, not like AI. It combines your personal source material (books, podcasts, videos, articles) with your authentic voice to produce content that is grounded in real ideas and written in your actual style.

The system has five main sections: Voice, Source Material, Content, Calendar, and Analytics.

---

## Voice

This is where you define who you are as a communicator. Everything the AI generates flows through your voice configuration.

### Your Voice Persona

A guided wizard walks you through a discovery process to capture your authentic voice. The questions are designed to bypass the two masks most people wear on social media: the brash, provocative persona or the polished, corporate persona. The output is a detailed voice profile that the AI uses every time it writes for you.

### Your Content Pillars

A second wizard helps you define 3-5 core topics that anchor your LinkedIn presence. The AI guides you through an internal audit (your expertise and convictions) and an external audit (your audience's needs and your business objectives) to find the intersection. These pillars ensure every post connects back to a coherent strategy.

### Your Hook Types

LinkedIn posts succeed or fail in the first two lines. The system includes a library of 30+ proven rhetorical hooks, organized by funnel stage (awareness, consideration, conversion). You select the 7 that best match your authentic style. The AI will only use hooks you've approved.

Some hook types (personal stories, vulnerable reflections) are reserved for posts you write yourself. The AI will never fabricate personal narratives.

---

## Source Material

This is your content library. Instead of generating content from nothing, the system generates from things you've actually read, listened to, and watched.

### Adding Sources

**Books** — Search Google Books by title or author, or connect your Hardcover reading account to browse books you've already read. When you add a book, the AI generates a deep summary including key concepts, table of contents, and related ideas.

**Podcasts** — Search iTunes for any podcast, browse episodes, and add the ones that sparked ideas. The system transcribes the audio and extracts a summary, key takeaways, chapter markers, and notable people and concepts mentioned.

**YouTube Videos** — Search YouTube or paste a URL. Same transcription and analysis as podcasts.

**Articles** — Paste any URL. The AI reads the page, summarizes it, and extracts the key points.

### Your Library

All ingested sources appear in your library, organized by type. Each source shows the AI-generated summary and key takeaways. You can hide sources you've exhausted or delete them permanently. A counter tracks how many times each source has been used to generate content.

---

## Content

This is where posts are created, refined, and published.

### Creating a Post

**From Source** — Choose a source from your library (a book, podcast episode, video, or article). Then select a content pillar, hook type, and funnel stage. The AI generates a draft that draws on your source material, applies your voice persona, and follows your strategic framework.

**From Scratch** — Start with a blank editor and write your own post. The AI checks your writing against your voice rules in real time (flagging banned words and patterns). You can generate visual assets (quote cards, carousel PDFs) from what you've written.

### The Editor

Every post has access to:

- **LinkedIn Preview** — Shows exactly where the "see more" fold will cut your post on desktop and mobile, so you can craft your hook to land right before it.
- **First Comment** — Write a comment that gets automatically posted 1-5 minutes after your main post. This is where you put links and calls to action, since LinkedIn penalizes posts with links in the body.
- **Visual Assets** — Generate a branded quote card (pulls the strongest line from your post) or a carousel PDF (AI structures your post into slides with your brand colors, fonts, and logo).
- **Voice Check** — Flags banned words and AI-writing patterns in real time as you type.

### Publishing

- **Post Now** — Publishes immediately to LinkedIn. If you have a first comment configured, it queues automatically.
- **Schedule** — Set a date and time. A background job publishes it when the time comes.
- **Carousel Posts** — If you've generated a carousel PDF, the system uploads it to LinkedIn as a document post with slide pagination.

---

## Calendar

A weekly view that shows your scheduled posts on a 7-day grid. Each post is color-coded by content pillar and funnel stage. The flywheel indicator at the top shows your progress against weekly targets (default: 4 awareness posts, 2 consideration posts, 1 conversion post per week). Unscheduled drafts appear below the calendar so you can drag your pipeline forward.

---

## Analytics

Performance data for published posts, pulled directly from LinkedIn.

**Metrics tracked:** impressions, likes, comments, shares, clicks, and engagement rate.

**Breakdowns by:**
- Content pillar (which topics perform best)
- Hook type (which opening patterns drive engagement)
- Funnel stage (awareness vs. consideration vs. conversion)

**Repurposing:** The system automatically identifies high-performing posts that are 60+ days old and flags them for regeneration from a new angle or format.

---

## How It All Connects

1. You define your voice, pillars, and preferred hooks in the **Voice** tab.
2. You build a library of source material in the **Source Material** tab.
3. You create posts in the **Content** tab, either from your library or from scratch.
4. You schedule and track posts in the **Calendar** tab.
5. You measure what's working in the **Analytics** tab.
6. High performers get recycled back into your source library for fresh takes.

The AI is a tool in service of your authentic voice. It never fabricates personal stories. It never uses patterns you've banned. It always defers to your strategy. The goal is content that sounds like the real you, grounded in things you've actually consumed, structured by a strategy you've defined.
